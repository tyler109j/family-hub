Our Family / Planner

Policy

Privacy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This is a private household planner intended only for Tyler and Kayla. It is not offered as a public service.

Information stored

The planner stores information the two family members choose to add, including events, tasks, shopping items, meal plans, household notes, account email addresses, and a history of planner changes.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the shared planner and fulfill requests made through the connected Family Assistant. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Services involved

  • Supabase provides authentication, database storage, live updates, and the protected planner API.
  • GitHub Pages serves the planner application. Private planner records are not stored in the website source.
  • OpenAI ChatGPT processes messages sent to the Family Assistant and calls the planner API after authorization. OpenAI's own privacy terms apply to ChatGPT use.

Access and security

Database access is restricted to the two approved family accounts. Each connected ChatGPT account receives its own authorization, and database rules are enforced for every request. Secret administrator credentials are not included in the website source.

Retention and deletion

Planner information remains until Tyler or Kayla removes it or the Family Planner project is deleted. Either family member may disconnect the Family Assistant from ChatGPT settings.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to Tyler109j@gmail.com.
