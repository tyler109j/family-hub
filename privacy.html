Our Family Planner

Privacy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This is a private household planner intended only for Tyler and Kayla. It is not offered as a public service.

Information stored

The planner stores the information the two family members choose to add, such as events, tasks, shopping items, meal plans, household notes, account email addresses, and a history of planner changes.

How information is used

Information is used only to display and operate the shared planner and to fulfill requests made through the connected Family Assistant. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Services involved

  • Supabase provides authentication, database storage, live updates, and the protected planner API.
  • GitHub Pages serves the planner’s public application files. Private planner records are not stored in the GitHub repository.
  • OpenAI ChatGPT processes the messages sent to the Family Assistant and calls the planner API after the user authorizes the connection. OpenAI’s own privacy terms apply to ChatGPT use.

Access and security

Database access is restricted to the two approved family accounts. Each connected ChatGPT account receives its own authorization, and database rules are enforced for every request. Secret administrator credentials are not included in the website or GitHub repository.

Retention and deletion

Planner information remains until Tyler or Kayla removes it or the Family Planner project is deleted. Either family member may disconnect the Family Assistant from ChatGPT settings.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to Tyler109j@gmail.com.
